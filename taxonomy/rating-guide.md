# What makes a Yes / No / Maybe-yes for CIQ

Imagery should look designed for CIQ and not copied from someone else's brand.
Licensed stock (Adobe, iStock) is fine when it fits the brand.

## Yes
- On-brand, polished, and usable today without edits.
- Clearly about CIQ's world: enterprise Linux, HPC, containers, automation,
  research and data work, infrastructure, security.
- Clean composition with room for text (heroes, backgrounds).
- Screenshots show real product UI, legible and current.
- Diagrams are readable at slide size.

## No
- Generic stock cliches: handshakes, glowing padlocks, people pointing at screens.
- Looks like another company's brand, or contains third-party logos.
- Cluttered, low resolution, watermarked, or visibly AI-garbled (bad hands,
  gibberish text).
- Off-topic for CIQ's products and audiences.
- Inspiration-only material (reference, never shipped).

## Maybe-yes
- Promising style or subject, but needs a crop, a fix, or a license check.
- On-brand mood but weak on subject, or the reverse.
- You would use it if a curator vouched for it.

## Strong vs unsure
- Strong: you would bet the curators agree.
- Unsure: anything borderline, or a style you have not seen them rule on.
